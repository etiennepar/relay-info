Relay

A privately operated assistant for a two-person household.

Relay helps its users work with email, files, calendars, research, and personal projects through private Telegram conversations. It connects to the Google accounts that each user authorizes and uses AI services to help carry out their requests and configured workflows.

Google access supports reading email, preparing and sending messages through supported workflows, reading and organizing Drive files, creating documents and other outputs, and reading and updating calendars. The actions available depend on the user's account permissions and the installed Relay features.

Relay is homemade software for its existing household users. It is not offered as a public service and has no public signup. This website provides application information only; it does not host the assistant, its credentials, or users' content.

Privacy and Google data use

Personal-use terms

Relay is provided as-is for the household's personal use. Its users choose the accounts, resources, requests, and workflows they use with it. AI-generated work can contain mistakes and should be reviewed as appropriate. Google and other connected services remain subject to their own terms.

Contact

Existing users can contact the household maintainer directly through their established private communication channel. Google authorization screens also provide the app's support contact.
